About us

Safeguarding laboratory medicine in Kenya

Medical laboratory professionals are central to accurate diagnosis and safe patient care. Our mandate is to regulate training, registration and practice so that every result reported in a Kenyan laboratory is produced by a competent, licensed professional.

We maintain the national register, accredit continuous professional development, and set the standards that laboratories are assessed against — while giving the public a simple way to check who they are dealing with.
